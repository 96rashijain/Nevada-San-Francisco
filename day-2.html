Golden, sharply eroded badlands ridges — one of Death Valley's signature photographs, and an easy roadside viewpoint just steps from parking. A short detour east of Furnace Creek — do this one first while it's cool and the light is good, then head back through Furnace Creek toward Badwater Road.

Artist's Drive / Artist's Palette Should 📷
A narrow one-way scenic loop through mineral hills streaked pink, green, yellow, and purple, branching off Badwater Road before you reach Badwater itself — drive it now so you continue straight on to Badwater afterward instead of backtracking. Skip only if you're already running late.

Badwater Basin Must 📷 🆓 with park pass
The lowest point in North America at −282 ft — a vast white salt-flat basin you walk straight onto from the parking area. Walk out for photos toward the mountains; you don't need to go far for the shot. 45–60 min. 🐦 Watch for ravens and other desert birds.

Death Valley's palette is exactly this — pale salt flats, sienna-and-purple mineral hills, badlands brown. Light, breathable, sun-reflective colors aren't just photogenic here, they matter: highs run ~90°F even in October. A wide-brim hat in bone white or sand reads well in every Badwater/Zabriskie photo and keeps the sun off.

🎒 Carry today

  • Several liters of water per person — non-negotiable in Death Valley
  • Wide-brim hat, sunglasses, sunscreen, lip balm
  • Full tank of gas leaving Vegas/Pahrump
  • Offline maps downloaded — cell signal is unreliable in the park

🍽️ Eat

  • Packed Jain lunch — carry it in, food options inside the park are thin and not Jain-reliable
  • Electrolytes alongside water
  • Dinner in Bakersfield: Vida Vegan Company or Asian Vegan Bistro (2217 Ashe Rd) — both dedicated vegan kitchens

➕ Optional enroute

  • Optional Mesquite Flat Dunes — drop first if behind
  • Should Artist's Drive/Palette — drop second if still behind
  • Skip Dante's View — not built into this itinerary; too big a detour for this day's timing

If you're behind — cut in this order

  1. Drop Mesquite Flat Sand Dunes first
  2. Drop Artist's Drive/Palette next
  3. Keep Zabriskie Point and Badwater Basin — non-negotiable
  4. Leave Death Valley by ~3 PM regardless — protect the Bakersfield arrival

🏨 Tonight's stay — Bakersfield

Target $80–100. Ordinary chain-motel pricing — plenty of options right off CA-99/CA-58.

Cheaper alternate: Delano, ~25 min further along your route toward Sequoia, sometimes has lower rates — only worth it if it doesn't add meaningfully to tomorrow's drive.

📌 Facts about today

  • Badwater Basin's −282 ft makes it the lowest point in North America — and on a clear day you can see Telescope Peak (11,043 ft) in the Panamint Range from the same spot, the largest elevation range visible in the contiguous US.
  • Death Valley holds the record for the hottest air temperature ever reliably recorded on Earth (134°F at Furnace Creek in 1913) — in October you're well past that risk, but it's why water discipline matters even on a "mild" day here.
  • Zabriskie Point's badlands were shaped by an ancient lakebed that dried up roughly 5 million years ago, then sculpted by wind and infrequent flash floods into the golden ridges you'll photograph.
