🎒 Master Packing & Car Checklist

Oct 10–19, 2026 · 2 travelers. This trip crosses desert, alpine, and coastal climates in ten days — the kit changes by region, not just by day.

Carry every day

Documents

  • Driver's license / photo ID
  • Rental car confirmation
  • Flight confirmations
  • Hotel confirmations
  • Physical America the Beautiful pass
  • Credit card used for the rental + a backup card

Electronics

  • Phones + charging cables
  • Car charger, power bank
  • Camera, spare batteries, memory card
  • Offline Google Maps downloaded for each region
  • Downloaded park maps (Death Valley, Sequoia, Yosemite)

In the car

  • Phone mount
  • Sunglasses (spare pair)
  • Paper towels, trash bags
  • Small cooler, reusable water bottles
  • Flashlight, emergency blanket, basic first-aid kit

Cooler / food

  • Jain sandwiches/wraps, fruit
  • Yogurt/curd where practical, nuts, protein snacks
  • Crackers, dry snacks, electrolytes
  • Water, napkins, disposable cutlery, wet wipes
Food-desert days — carry a full lunch, not just snacks: Oct 11 (Death Valley), Oct 15 (Tioga), Oct 18 (Big Sur). Buy what you need the evening or morning before each — see the "buy ahead" notes on the Budget page.

Climate kits

☀️ Desert kit — Oct 10–11 (Vegas, Death Valley)

Wide-brim hat, sunglasses, sunscreen, lip balm, breathable shirt, light pants/shorts, several liters of water per person, electrolytes, small backpack.

🏔️ Sierra kit — Oct 12–16 (Sequoia, Yosemite, Tioga, Tahoe)

Fleece, insulated jacket, warm hat, gloves, long pants, closed-toe shoes, optional hiking shoes, warm socks. Do not assume California will be warm here — Tioga Pass can be dramatically colder than Yosemite Valley the same morning.

🌊 Coast kit — Oct 17–19 (Napa, Big Sur, San Francisco)

Windbreaker, light sweater, comfortable shoes, sunglasses, layers. Big Sur and San Francisco can feel much colder than the actual temperature suggests, because of wind and fog.

Hiking

You do not need serious hiking gear for this trip. Required: comfortable walking shoes. Optional: trail shoes, hiking socks, a small daypack. Potential walks along the route: Big Trees Trail, General Sherman, Moro Rock's staircase, the Mist Trail footbridge, Partington Cove if you add it.

Wildlife gear

  • Binoculars or a phone with real zoom
  • Camera zoom lens, if you already have one
  • Never approach wildlife
  • Never feed wildlife
  • Keep food secured in the car, not left out at overlooks

Before every long drive

Morning checklist

  • Gas
  • Water and food
  • Phone charged, offline map loaded
  • Weather and road conditions checked
  • Hotel address saved, next fuel stop identified

Days that need an extra check

  • Oct 15: Tioga Road status — nps.gov/yose or 209-372-0200
  • Oct 18: Caltrans Highway 1 + Big Sur/McWay status
