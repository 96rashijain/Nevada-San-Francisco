17-Mile Drive Must 📷 🦭
$12.50/vehicle, gates open sunrise–sunset. The fee is reimbursed with a qualifying $35+ purchase at participating Pebble Beach restaurants — don't count on the reimbursement unless you actually make that purchase. Enter at the Pacific Grove Gate and follow the stops roughly in this order: (1) Spanish Bay → (2) Point Joe → (3) Bird Rock → (4) Cypress Point → (5) Lone Cypress → (6) The Restless Sea → (7) Crocker Grove → exit at the Carmel Gate.
17-Mile Drive — Pacific Grove Gate

Spanish Bay — Must

Beach and boardwalk, a good stretch-your-legs stop early in the drive.

Point Joe — Must, Photo

Dramatic rocky coastline where converging currents create visibly rough water just offshore.

Bird Rock — Must, Wildlife

Seabirds and sometimes seals/sea lions are visible on and around the rock — the drive's clearest deliberate wildlife stop.

Lone Cypress — Must, Photo

The famous isolated Monterey cypress perched on a granite outcrop — one of the most photographed trees in California.

Restless Sea — Must, Photo

Dramatic waves crashing against rocks where two currents meet, right off the road.

Crocker Grove — Should

Stand of giant Monterey cypress trees, a quieter stop compared to the coastal viewpoints.

Coastal blue and fog gray match Big Sur's cliffs-and-marine-layer look and carry straight into San Francisco's evening fog. Dress in real layers today — Monterey/Big Sur mornings are often foggy and cool, and SF adds wind on top of that by evening.

🎒 Carry today

  • Windbreaker — Big Sur and SF are both windier than the trip so far
  • $12.50 cash/card ready for the 17-Mile Drive gate
  • Full lunch + snacks — don't rely on finding a Jain meal in Big Sur
  • Caltrans QuickMap checked before you leave Napa

🍽️ Eat

  • Lunch: Pizza My Heart (vegan pizza) or Ambrosia India Cuisine, Monterey
  • Backup snacks for the Big Sur stretch — don't rely on finding food there
  • Dinner: North Beach Pizza, San Francisco — vegan cheese available

➕ Optional enroute

  • Optional Carmel-by-the-Sea — only if ahead
  • Optional Partington Cove, McWay Falls — both condition/time dependent
  • Optional Coit Tower — energy-dependent, end of a very long day

If you're behind — cut in this order

  1. Drop Carmel-by-the-Sea first
  2. Drop Partington Cove and McWay Falls next (McWay may already be closed anyway)
  3. Drop Coit Tower and Crocker Grove if the day is still running long
  4. Never cut 17-Mile Drive, Bixby Creek Bridge, or Pier 39 — those are the day's true anchors
  5. If traffic is bad, Bunny Cafe is the first evening item to move or cancel — rebook if needed

🏨 Tonight's stay — San Francisco

Target $90–120. Staying near North Beach/the Wharf keeps tonight's walk short; staying near the Golden Gate/Marina side sets up tomorrow morning better instead — pick based on which matters more to you.

Cheaper alternate: South San Francisco, San Bruno, or Millbrae, near SFO, run noticeably cheaper and are only ~20–25 min from downtown — a good option if tonight's neighborhood doesn't matter much to you.

📌 Facts about today

  • The Lone Cypress is estimated to be over 250 years old and has been held in place for decades partly by cables and support systems, since erosion has steadily undercut its original rock perch.
  • Bixby Creek Bridge was completed in 1932 and was, at the time, one of the tallest single-span concrete bridges in the world — it made the previously multi-day inland detour around Big Sur unnecessary.
  • Pier 39's sea lions showed up on their own in 1989, shortly after the Loma Prieta earthquake, and simply never left — what started as a handful of animals grew into a population that's sometimes numbered in the hundreds.
