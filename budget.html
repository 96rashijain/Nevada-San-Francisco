Gas

Don't budget purely off the earlier mileage estimate — use ~1,400+ planning miles for the full loop rather than an older 1,290–1,330 figure, since the route has been adjusted since that was first estimated. At ~25–30 mpg, that's roughly 47–56 gallons; at a blended ~$4–5/gallon, that's ~$190–280. Use $250 as your working number.

America the Beautiful pass

Your existing pass should cover federal park entrance fees for Death Valley, Sequoia, and Yosemite — a real part of why this route works economically. It does not cover Hoover Dam, Emerald Bay State Park, 17-Mile Drive, Bunny Cafe, or any private attraction — those are state, county, or privately run and bill separately.

San Francisco — free vs. paid

🆓 Free

  • Palace of Fine Arts
  • Crissy Field
  • Golden Gate Bridge walk
  • Chinatown, North Beach
  • Pier 39 + sea lions
  • Lombard Street

💰 Small paid extras

  • Bunny Cafe Lounge — the one paid experience worth deliberately keeping, since it's specifically wanted
  • Coit Tower interior — optional, skip unless you want it
  • Battery Spencer — $10.50 toll on the way back into SF, not an entry fee — Fort Point is the free alternative

📌 Money-saving rules

  • Keep the America the Beautiful pass in the glovebox — three parks covered
  • Skip unnecessary paid park tours (Hoover Dam especially)
  • Skip a Vegas gondola ride, High Roller, or SlotZilla unless you specifically want one
  • Pack every desert/Tioga/Big Sur lunch rather than relying on finding food there
  • Don't pay for wine tasting in Napa — the scenery is free

Food budget

For two: grocery/cooler $100–150, restaurants $150–250, total $250–400. The Jain diet makes grocery/cooler food especially useful on the three food-desert days (Death Valley, Tioga, Big Sur) — budget for a real grocery run before each.

Buy before Death Valley (Day 2)

In Las Vegas or Pahrump: water, fruit, sandwich ingredients, yogurt, snacks, electrolytes, ice, napkins, drinks.

Buy before Tioga (Day 6)

In Yosemite/Mariposa/Oakhurst: full lunch, water, snacks, fruit, drinks. Don't assume you can buy food in the high Sierra.

Buy before Big Sur (Day 9)

In Monterey/Carmel: full lunch, water, snacks, a dinner backup. Don't rely on Big Sur restaurants for a Jain meal.
