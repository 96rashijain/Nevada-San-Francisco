Las Vegas — Hoover Dam & the Strip

Keep it easy after the flight. Hoover Dam by mid-morning, then the free Strip sights at your own pace — this is the day to see the Strip, since tonight is your only night in Vegas and tomorrow starts early.

Hoover Dam Must Free to walk 📷
Park at the Visitor Center lot and walk: the bridge viewpoint, the dam top, and the Colorado River viewpoints below. That combination alone is a complete visit — you don't need a ticketed tour.
Hoover Dam Visitor Center

Mike O'Callaghan–Pat Tillman Memorial Bridge viewpoint

The pedestrian overlook on the bypass bridge, ~1,500 ft downstream from the dam. This is the elevated, full-dam-and-canyon photo — most people's favorite shot of the day. 15–20 min.

Mike O'Callaghan–Pat Tillman Memorial Bridge

Hoover Dam top

Walk across the dam itself — Lake Mead on one side, the Colorado River more than 700 ft below on the other. Free, no ticket needed for the walk. 30–40 min.

Paid options (skip unless you specifically want them)

Visitor Center exhibit ~$15, Powerplant Tour ~$25 (book ahead), Dam Tour ~$40 (walk-up only). None are covered by an America the Beautiful pass — Hoover Dam runs its own separate fee system. You have far more dramatic scenery later in this trip; we wouldn't spend the money here.

The Strip Should Free 📷
This block is the actual plan for the afternoon, not filler — you land at 8 AM, Hoover Dam is done by noon, and you're already sleeping in Vegas tonight, so this is the one afternoon you have for it. It's tagged Should rather than Must only because, unlike Hoover Dam, it's genuinely droppable without losing something unique to this route — if the day runs long, this is what flexes. Every stop below is listed on its own so you know exactly what each one is and what to drop first — walk them in this order: Bellagio Fountains → Bellagio Conservatory → Paris Las Vegas exterior → Caesars Palace / Forum Shops → The Venetian → Sphere exterior. Fremont Street and High Roller are farther out — only if you still have energy after that core walk.

Bellagio Fountains Should Free 📷

Choreographed fountains directly in front of the Bellagio, right on the Strip. Over 1,000 individual jets set to music; shows run every 15–30 min in the afternoon/evening. Stay for at least one full show. 20–30 min.

Bellagio Fountains

Bellagio Conservatory & Botanical Garden Optional Free 📷

Free indoor floral display just inside the Bellagio lobby, redone seasonally by a full horticulture team. Good option if it's hot outside, and an easy add since you're at the Bellagio anyway for the fountains. 15–25 min.

Paris Las Vegas exterior Should Free 📷

The half-scale Eiffel Tower replica and Arc de Triomphe facade right on the Strip sidewalk, directly across from Bellagio — a two-minute walk-by, no reason to skip it. Photo only; you don't need to pay for the tower's observation deck.

Paris Las Vegas

Caesars Palace / Forum Shops Should Free 📷

Roman-columned exterior and porte-cochère outside, and inside the Forum Shops a painted sky ceiling, classical statuary, and its own free fountain show at the Fountain of the Gods. Worth walking through even if you're not shopping. 20–30 min.

Caesars Palace / Forum Shops

The Venetian Should Free

Indoor Italian-themed resort with painted ceilings and indoor canals lined with shops and cafes. You don't need to pay for a gondola ride to enjoy walking through. 30–45 min.

The Venetian Resort

Sphere exterior Optional Free 📷

The giant LED-wrapped globe near the Venetian — its programmed exterior display (eyeballs, planets, ads, whatever's running that week) is genuinely striking and free to view from outside. Looks best after dark, so it fits better if your Strip walk runs into early evening than if you're doing this block at 2 PM. No need to go inside or see a show.

Sphere

Fremont Street Experience Optional

A pedestrian district downtown under a giant LED canopy, distinct from the Strip's resort-casino vibe — a genuine detour, not on the same walk as the rest of this list. 45–60 min if you go. Paid extra: SlotZilla zipline ~$55–75/person — skip unless you specifically want it.

High Roller observation wheel Optional

~$25–37/person depending on time of day. A good view, but not necessary — you'll get comparable elevated views for free elsewhere on this trip.

  1. Drop High Roller / SlotZilla / any paid attraction first — you don't need them
  2. Drop the Sphere exterior next — it's a quick add, not a reason to reroute if you're short on time
  3. Drop Fremont Street after that if energy is low — do it after dinner only if you still want more Vegas, never at the cost of dinner or rest

📌 Facts about today

  • Hoover Dam sits almost exactly on the Nevada–Arizona border, and the bypass bridge you'll view it from is named for two Medal-of-Honor-era public figures: Nevada governor Mike O'Callaghan and NFL player-turned-Army-Ranger Pat Tillman.
  • The Bellagio fountains use over 1,000 individual jets choreographed to music — the water can shoot as high as ~460 feet on the tallest sequences.
  • Hoover Dam generates hydroelectric power for parts of Nevada, Arizona, and California, but the dam and its tours are self-funded through admission and power revenue — not the National Park Service, which is why your America the Beautiful pass doesn't apply here.
