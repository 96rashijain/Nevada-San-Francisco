📌 Facts about today

  • The Ahwahnee opened in 1927 and was built with local granite and concrete stained and textured to look like redwood, partly to satisfy fire-safety rules while keeping the rustic look architects wanted.
  • Vernal Fall drops about 317 ft in a single, mostly unbroken sheet — the mist that gives the Mist Trail its name is strong enough to soak hikers even from a distance in spring, though by October flow is much gentler.
  • Yosemite Valley itself was carved by glaciers, not rivers — the classic U-shaped cross-section you see from Tunnel View and Valley View is a textbook glacial-valley profile.
