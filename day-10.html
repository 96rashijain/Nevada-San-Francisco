Fort Point Should 🆓 📷
A brick Civil War-era fort directly under the bridge's south end, looking straight up at the span from water level — a genuinely different, closer-up postcard angle than Crissy Field, free, and it costs you no toll and barely any detour since it's a couple minutes from the Welcome Center. A better use of your remaining time than Battery Spencer if you have to pick one.

Battery Spencer Optional 📷
The classic elevated postcard view of the bridge from the Marin side — but getting there means crossing the bridge (free northbound) and coming back south, which means paying the toll: $10.50 if you don't have a FasTrak/plate account set up in advance (confirmed current rate via goldengatebridgetoll.com). Skip it if airport timing is getting tight or you'd rather not pay for the view — Fort Point gets you a comparable photo for free.

One deliberate pop of "International Orange" against fog navy and morning mist is the classic Golden Gate photo palette — the bridge's actual color reads as warm red-orange against the cool fog behind it. Dress warm; SF mornings run cooler and windier than anywhere else on the trip.

📌 Facts about today

  • The Golden Gate Bridge's signature color, "International Orange," was chosen partly because it complements the natural surroundings and stands out in fog far better than standard gray or black would have.
  • The Palace of Fine Arts is the sole surviving full structure from the 1915 Panama-Pacific International Exposition — it was originally meant to be temporary and was rebuilt in permanent materials decades later specifically because San Franciscans didn't want to lose it.
  • San Francisco's summer-into-fall fog (locally nicknamed "Karl") is driven by the same Central Valley heat that makes Napa and Death Valley hot — cool coastal air gets pulled inland to replace rising hot air, condensing into fog as it crosses the cold California Current just offshore.
