How to read every page

Each stop on every day's timeline is tagged with one of four levels. This replaces the old "optional" label, which was hiding genuinely important stops (like the Strip) next to genuinely skippable ones. Now the tag alone tells you what to do if you're behind schedule.

Must— do this. Build the day around it. Should— do it if you're on schedule. Optional— nice add-on, first thing to cut. Skip— not worth it on this trip.
🐻 Wildlife spot 📷 Photo spot 🆓 Free ⚠️ Check condition before you go

Rule of thumb for every day: on schedule → do Must + Should. 30–60 min behind → Must only. More than an hour behind → the first Musts on the list, then head straight to the hotel. Never trade safety for a stop — if weather or road conditions turn, skip it.
